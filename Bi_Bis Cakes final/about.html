Our Story

Bi_Bi's Cakes is a South African cupcake business that specialises in freshly baked and beautifully designed cupcakes.

The business was created to provide customers with delicious and affordable cupcakes for birthdays, weddings, baby showers, celebrations and other special events.

We aim to make every celebration more memorable by providing cupcakes that look as good as they taste.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide high-quality, freshly baked and beautifully designed cupcakes that make every celebration memorable.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted local cupcake brand known for creativity, quality and excellent customer service.

  • Businesses
  • Customers planning special occasions
